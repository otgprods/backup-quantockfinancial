We offer whole of market, bespoke mortgage advice for first charge mortgages to suit a wide range of clients. We can help with a residential purchase, remortgage, first-time buyers, buy-to-let, house in multiple occupation (HMO) and holiday properties.

Choosing a mortgage can be daunting. A product with the lowest rate isn’t always the best deal. Additional arrangement fees or a valuation to pay can make a mortgage much more expensive than one with a higher rate that has no fees. We consider all the options to find the right deal for you.

Protection

Protecting the ones that matter

At Quantock Financial, we understand that personal protection is about securing the future of those who depend on you financially. The aim of financial protection is to ensure that, in the event of your death or serious illness, your loved ones are provided with the financial support they need.

It’s important to consider the potential impact and consequences your absence or ill health could have on those closest to you. By planning ahead with the right protection solutions, you can safeguard your family or dependants from financial uncertainty, ensuring that they are taken care of during life’s most challenging times.

Learn More about protection

Insurance

Giving you peace of mind

Insurance is key to securing your financial future and protecting loved ones. Life insurance provides a lump sum or income upon death to cover debts or living expenses. Critical illness cover offers financial support if diagnosed with a serious condition, while Family Income Benefit provides ongoing payments for dependants.

Income protection safeguards your earnings if you can’t work due to illness or injury, and buildings and contents insurance shields your home and belongings from theft or natural disasters.

Learn More about insurance

Supporting our heroes

Supporting NHS heroes with tailored financial solutions

At Quantock Financial, we proudly support NHS employees by offering tailored financial services through Health Staff Discounts. We work closely with NHS staff across the country, understanding the unique challenges and opportunities they face.

Our goal is to provide expert financial advice and solutions that help them secure a brighter financial future, whether it’s through personal protection, savings, or retirement planning. We’re here to ensure that those who take care of others are well taken care of themselves.
