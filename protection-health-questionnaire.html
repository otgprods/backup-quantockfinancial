Client questionnaire

For your life insurance, critical illness and income protection application

Now that you have your mortgage offer and our protection recommendation, the next step is to apply to the insurer. These questions cover the health and lifestyle details insurers ask about, so we can complete your application accurately and avoid delays.

How to fill it in

  • Fill in the form below and press Submit when you have finished.
  • Applying with someone else? Choose Joint application and answer for both of you.
  • It takes around 10 minutes.

When you have finished

  • Press Submit and your answers will be sent securely to our office.
  • Not sure about something? Leave it blank and we will go through it together.

Honest answers protect your cover

Please answer every question fully and honestly, even if something seems minor or happened a long time ago. If information given to an insurer is missing or incorrect, it could reduce a claim or mean a claim is not paid. If in doubt, tell us.
